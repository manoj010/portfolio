# Why MDX is enough for this blog

For a personal portfolio, the best blogging system is the one that does not get in the way of writing.

MDX keeps the posts close to the codebase. I can write in the editor, commit the post, deploy the site, and share the link. No database, no admin panel, no auth flow.

## When I would change this

I would reach for a database or CMS if the blog needed comments, multiple authors, scheduled publishing, drafts, or a web editor.

Until then, files are a good place for thoughts to live.
